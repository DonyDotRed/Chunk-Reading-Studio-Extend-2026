## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> A research article is often described by its structure: introduction, methods, results, and discussion. This description is accurate but incomplete. Beneath the structure lies an argument: a claim supported by evidence, connected to that evidence by reasoning, and limited by stated qualifications. This essay uses the three studies developed in this track to show how each section of an article contributes to a single argument.
>
> The philosopher Stephen Toulmin proposed a model of practical argument that is useful here. In his account, a claim is supported by data, the data are connected to the claim by a warrant, the warrant may itself require backing, and the claim is often limited by a qualifier and exposed to possible rebuttals. Although Toulmin developed the model for everyday reasoning rather than for science, its elements correspond closely to the sections of a research article.
>
> The introduction establishes why a claim is needed. In the eye lens study, the revised dose limit and the uncertain adequacy of trunk-worn dosimetry created a question that existing knowledge could not answer. The introduction does not state the claim itself; it creates the space in which a claim becomes necessary.
>
> The methods section provides the warrant and its backing. A ratio of Hp(3) to Hp(10) can count as evidence about lens dose only if the dosimeters were calibrated traceably, their response verified in relevant fields, and values below the detection limit handled appropriately. Readers who distrust these procedures will reject the conclusion regardless of the numbers.
>
> The results supply the data, reported without interpretation so that readers can judge them independently. The discussion then states the claim, attaches qualifiers, and addresses rebuttals. In the glycemic study, the claim that discordance carries prognostic significance was qualified by its observational design, and an unexpected null finding was addressed by acknowledging its wide confidence interval. In the retrieval study, a section on limitations anticipated the rebuttal that data contamination might explain the results.
>
> The same argument is then re-presented for different audiences. The abstract compresses it for researchers deciding whether to read further; the cover letter frames its significance for an editor; the response to reviewers defends and refines it; the presentation script makes it audible; and the plain-language summary translates it for those affected by it. In each version, the claim remains the same, but its qualifiers must survive the change of form. A version that drops them, such as a press summary claiming that sensors "prevent complications," has changed the argument rather than simplified it.
>
> Seen in this way, writing a research article is less a matter of filling sections than of building a case that a skeptical reader can follow and test. The conventions examined throughout this track, from the moves of an introduction to the hedges of a discussion, are the tools by which that case is made both persuasive and honest.

어휘: count as ~로 간주되다 · traceably 소급성 있게 · appropriately 적절히

**⑬ Readers who distrust these procedures will reject the conclusion regardless of the numbers.**
이 절차를 믿지 않는 독자는 숫자가 어떻든 결론을 받아들이지 않을 것이다.
어휘: distrust 불신하다 · reject 거부하다

### 문단 5

**⑭ The results supply the data, reported without interpretation so that readers can judge them independently.**
결과는 자료를 제공하며, 독자가 독립적으로 판단할 수 있도록 해석 없이 보고한다.
어휘: supply 제공하다

**⑮ The discussion then states the claim, attaches qualifiers, and addresses rebuttals.**
논의는 그다음 주장을 진술하고, 한정어를 붙이고, 반박에 대응한다.
어휘: attach 붙이다 · address 대응하다

**⑯ In the glycemic study, the claim that discordance carries prognostic significance was qualified by its observational design, and an unexpected null finding was addressed by acknowledging its wide confidence interval.**
혈당 연구에서는 불일치가 예후적 의미를 갖는다는 주장에 관찰 연구라는 설계상의 단서가 붙었고, 예상치 못한 유의하지 않은 결과는 넓은 신뢰구간을 인정하는 방식으로 다루어졌다.
어휘: qualify 단서를 붙이다 · observational 관찰의 · null finding 유의하지 않은 결과

**⑰ In the retrieval study, a section on limitations anticipated the rebuttal that data contamination might explain the results.**
검색 연구에서는 한계 절이 데이터 오염으로 결과를 설명할 수 있다는 반박에 미리 대비했다.
어휘: anticipate 예상하다, 미리 대비하다

### 문단 6

**⑱ The same argument is then re-presented for different audiences.**
같은 논증은 이어서 서로 다른 청중을 위해 다시 제시된다.
어휘: re-present 다시 제시하다

**⑲ The abstract compresses it for researchers deciding whether to read further; the cover letter frames its significance for an editor; the response to reviewers defends and refines it; the presentation script makes it audible; and the plain-language summary translates it for those affected by it.**
초록은 더 읽을지 결정하는 연구자를 위해 논증을 압축하고, 투고 서신은 편집자를 위해 그 중요성을 짜 맞추며, 심사 답변서는 논증을 방어하고 다듬는다. 발표 원고는 논증을 귀로 들을 수 있게 만들고, 쉬운 말 요약은 연구의 영향을 받는 사람들을 위해 그것을 옮긴다.
어휘: compress 압축하다 · frame 틀을 짜다 · significance 중요성 · refine 다듬다 · audible 들리는 · translate 옮기다 · affect 영향을 미치다

**⑳ In each version, the claim remains the same, but its qualifiers must survive the change of form.**
판본마다 주장은 그대로이지만, 그 한정어는 형식이 바뀌어도 살아남아야 한다.
어휘: version 판본 · survive 살아남다

**㉑ A version that drops them, such as a press summary claiming that sensors "prevent complications," has changed the argument rather than simplified it.**
센서가 "합병증을 예방한다"고 주장하는 언론 요약처럼 한정어를 빼 버린 판본은 논증을 단순화한 것이 아니라 바꾼 것이다.
어휘: drop 빼다 · press 언론 · simplify 단순화하다

### 문단 7

**㉒ Seen in this way, writing a research article is less a matter of filling sections than of building a case that a skeptical reader can follow and test.**
이렇게 보면 연구 논문을 쓰는 일은 절을 채우는 일이라기보다, 회의적인 독자가 따라가며 검증할 수 있는 논거를 쌓는 일이다.
어휘: (Being) Seen 분사구문 · build a case 논거를 세우다 · skeptical 회의적인

**㉓ The conventions examined throughout this track, from the moves of an introduction to the hedges of a discussion, are the tools by which that case is made both persuasive and honest.**
서론의 수사 단계에서 논의의 완곡 표현까지, 이 트랙에서 살펴본 관례들은 그 논거를 설득력 있으면서도 정직하게 만드는 도구다.
어휘: convention 관례 · move (수사적) 단계 · hedge 완곡 표현 · persuasive 설득력 있는

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 구조 설명에서 기능 설명으로

> A research article is often described by its structure… **This description is accurate but incomplete.** Beneath the structure lies an argument…

"정확하지만 불완전하다(accurate but incomplete)"는 기존 설명을 부정하지 않고 확장할 때 쓰는 표현입니다. U06의 이분법 해체, D11의 "extend"와 같은 계열의 학술적 태도입니다.

### 2. 장르가 바뀌어도 한정어는 살아남아야 한다

| 장르 | 같은 주장 | 한정어가 살아 있는가 |
|---|---|---|
| 논의 (D11) | was associated with a higher risk… observational design… | ✅ |
| 투고 서신 (D14) | had a higher adjusted hazard (HR 1.46; 95% CI…) | ✅ |
| 쉬운 말 요약 (D17) | were more likely… This study shows a link, not proof… | ✅ |
| 가상의 언론 요약 | sensors "prevent complications" | ❌ 인과 주장으로 바뀜 |

이 시리즈가 University Track(U04 calibration)부터 강조해 온 원칙의 최종 적용입니다. 주장을 쉽게, 짧게, 말로 바꿀 때도 확신의 정도는 원래 근거에 맞춰 유지해야 합니다.
