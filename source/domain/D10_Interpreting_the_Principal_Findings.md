## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> This study found that, for most maintenance tasks during a refueling outage, lens doses estimated from trunk-worn dosimeters calibrated in Hp(10) were close to Hp(3) measured near the eyes. The median ratio Hp(3)/Hp(10) was 1.08, and lens doses were low relative to the annual limit of 20 mSv.
>
> The close agreement between the two quantities is consistent with the characteristics of the radiation field. In fields dominated by high-energy photons such as those from cobalt-60, the conversion coefficients for Hp(3) and Hp(10) are similar, so a ratio close to unity is expected when the eye and the trunk are exposed to similar fluence. The spread of ratios around this value most likely reflects differences in geometry, such as the worker's posture and the position of the source relative to the head and chest.
>
> The exceptions are informative. All six pairs with a ratio above 1.5 involved valve maintenance near contaminated surfaces, and in each case Hp(0.07)/Hp(10) was also elevated. This pattern suggests a contribution from beta radiation or low-energy photons, to which trunk-worn Hp(10) dosimetry is largely insensitive. Because these tasks were few and the radiation fields were not characterized spectrometrically, however, this explanation should be regarded as provisional.
>
> These findings have practical implications for lens dose monitoring. For the majority of maintenance tasks examined, routine whole-body dosimetry appears to provide an adequate basis for demonstrating compliance with the revised limit: although Hp(10) slightly underestimated Hp(3) on average, the difference was small relative to the margin between observed doses and the limit. Even the highest single-outage value of 3.2 mSv, if repeated in two outages per year, would correspond to less than one-third of the annual limit; contractors, however, who work at several units in succession may accumulate higher annual doses, which highlights the importance of dose records that follow the individual across sites. Dedicated eye dosimetry may be warranted, however, for tasks involving work close to contaminated components, where the ratio may substantially exceed unity. A graded approach, in which eye dosimeters are issued on the basis of task characteristics rather than to all workers, may therefore offer a reasonable balance between protection and practicality.
>
> These interpretations, however, rest on data from a single outage at one plant, and their generalizability is considered in the following section.

**⑭ A graded approach, in which eye dosimeters are issued on the basis of task characteristics rather than to all workers, may therefore offer a reasonable balance between protection and practicality.**
따라서 수정체 선량계를 모든 작업자에게가 아니라 작업 특성에 따라 지급하는 차등적 접근이 방호와 실용성 사이의 합리적인 균형이 될 수 있다.
어휘: graded approach 차등적 접근 · balance 균형 · practicality 실용성

### 문단 5

**⑮ These interpretations, however, rest on data from a single outage at one plant, and their generalizability is considered in the following section.**
그러나 이 해석들은 한 발전소의 단 한 번 정비 기간 자료에 기대고 있으며, 그 일반화 가능성은 다음 절에서 다룬다.
어휘: rest on ~에 기대다 · generalizability 일반화 가능성

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 논의 절의 표준 순서

| 문단 | 기능 | 이 글의 핵심 표현 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 주요 결과 재진술 (수치 최소화) | **This study found that**… |
| 2 | 주요 결과의 기전적 설명 | **is consistent with** the characteristics of the radiation field… **is expected when**… **most likely reflects**… |
| 3 | 예외 사례의 해석 + 해석의 확신 수준 | **The exceptions are informative.** … **This pattern suggests**… **should be regarded as provisional** |
| 4 | 실무적 함의 (조건부) | **These findings have practical implications for**… **appears to provide**… **although**… **relative to the margin**… **may be warranted**… |
| 5 | 한계로의 전환 | **These interpretations, however, rest on**… **is considered in the following section** |

D07(결과)에서 일부러 하지 않았던 해석, 즉 6쌍의 높은 비가 베타선이나 저에너지 광자 때문일 수 있다는 해석이 여기 3문단에서 처음 등장합니다. 결과 절과 논의 절의 역할 분담이 이렇게 이루어집니다.

### 2. 해석의 확신 수준을 조절하는 표현

| 확신 수준 | 표현 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 높음 (물리적으로 예상됨) | is expected | a ratio close to unity **is expected** |
| 중상 | most likely reflects | The spread… **most likely reflects** differences in geometry |
| 중간 | suggests | This pattern **suggests** a contribution from beta radiation… |
| 낮춤 (근거의 한계 명시) | should be regarded as provisional | this explanation **should be regarded as provisional** |

3문단은 해석을 제시하자마자 "however"로 그 한계(작업 수가 적고 분광 측정이 없었음)를 밝힙니다. 해석과 그 단서를 한 문단 안에 두는 것이 논의 절의 기본 습관입니다.

### 3. 실무적 함의를 조건부로 제시하기

| 표현 | 기능 |
|---|---|
| **appears to provide** an adequate basis | 단정 대신 완곡 |
| **although** Hp(10) slightly underestimated Hp(3) on average, the difference was small **relative to the margin** between observed doses and the limit | 근거의 약점과 그 크기를 함께 제시 |
| Dedicated eye dosimetry **may be warranted**, however, **for tasks involving**… | 예외 조건 |
| A graded approach… **may therefore offer** a reasonable balance | 정책 제안의 완곡 |

규제나 실무에 영향을 줄 수 있는 결론일수록 적용 조건과 근거의 약점을 함께 밝혀야 합니다. 중앙값 비 1.08은 몸통 Hp(10)이 눈의 Hp(3)을 평균 약 8% **과소평가**한다는 뜻이므로, 전신 선량 측정을 "보수적(conservative)"이라고 부를 수는 없습니다. 이 문장은 그 약점을 인정한 뒤, 그 크기가 한도까지의 여유에 비해 작다는 근거로 "적절하다"는 판단을 정당화합니다.

### 4. 분야 어휘

| 영어 | 한국어 | 영어 | 한국어 |
|---|---|---|---|
| close to unity | 1에 가까운 | spectrometric characterization | 분광 측정에 의한 규명 |
| fluence | 플루언스 | margin (to the limit) | (한도까지의) 여유 |
| geometry | 기하 조건 | dedicated eye dosimetry | 전용 수정체 선량 측정 |
| posture | 자세 | graded approach | 차등적 접근 |
| insensitive to | ~에 둔감한 | generalizability | 일반화 가능성 |
