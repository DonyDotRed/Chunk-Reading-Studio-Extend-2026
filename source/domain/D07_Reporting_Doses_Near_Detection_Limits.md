## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> **Measurements obtained**
>
> During one refueling outage, eye lens dosimeters were issued to 42 workers engaged in six categories of maintenance tasks. The outage lasted 34 days, and each worker wore a separate dosimeter during each of four consecutive work phases. A total of 168 paired measurements of Hp(3) and Hp(10) were obtained, and none were lost or damaged.
>
> **Values below the detection limit**
>
> Of the 168 Hp(3) measurements, 62 (37%) were below the detection limit of 0.02 mSv. These values are reported as "< 0.02 mSv" in Table 2 rather than as zero. Because a substantial proportion of the data were censored, summary statistics were estimated by regression on order statistics rather than by substituting a fixed value such as half the detection limit.
>
> **Distribution of lens doses**
>
> The estimated median Hp(3) per worker over the outage was 0.11 mSv (interquartile range, 0.03–0.34 mSv). The highest value, recorded for a worker performing valve maintenance, was 3.2 ± 0.4 mSv (expanded uncertainty, k = 2). No worker exceeded 5 mSv during the outage.
>
> **Doses by task category**
>
> Lens doses varied considerably across task categories (Table 3). The highest median values were observed for valve maintenance (0.42 mSv) and steam generator work (0.31 mSv), whereas median doses for scaffolding and insulation work were below the detection limit. Within each category, dose distributions were right-skewed, with a small number of workers accounting for a large share of the collective dose: the five workers with the highest Hp(3) values received 58% of the total lens dose recorded during the outage.
>
> **Comparison with whole-body dosimetry**
>
> For the 106 pairs in which both values exceeded the detection limit, the ratio Hp(3)/Hp(10) had a median of 1.08 (interquartile range, 0.94–1.21). The ratio exceeded 1.5 in 6 of the 106 pairs (6%), all of which were associated with valve maintenance near contaminated surfaces. In these cases, the ratio Hp(0.07)/Hp(10) was also elevated, ranging from 1.9 to 3.4.

### 3. 검출 한계 미만 값을 다루는 표현

| 표현 | 의미 |
|---|---|
| were below the detection limit of 0.02 mSv | 검출 한계 미만 |
| are reported as "< 0.02 mSv" rather than as zero | 0으로 보고하지 않음 (0은 "없음"으로 오해될 수 있음) |
| a substantial proportion of the data were censored | 중도절단 자료 |
| estimated by regression on order statistics rather than by substituting a fixed value such as half the detection limit | 대체법 대신 통계적 추정을 썼음을 명시 |

검출 한계 미만 값을 0이나 검출 한계의 절반으로 바꿔 넣으면 요약 통계량이 편향될 수 있습니다. 중도절단 비율이 클수록 이 선택이 결과에 큰 영향을 미치므로, 결과 절에서 어떤 방법을 썼는지 다시 짧게 밝히는 것이 좋습니다.
