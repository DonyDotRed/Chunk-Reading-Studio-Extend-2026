## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> **Dosimeters and positioning**
>
> Lens doses were measured using passive thermoluminescent dosimeters (TLDs) designed for the measurement of Hp(3). Each dosimeter contained a lithium fluoride detector (LiF:Mg,Cu,P) in a holder that was attached to a headband and positioned at the side of the head closest to the main radiation source, as close to the eye as practicable. Workers simultaneously wore their routine whole-body dosimeter on the chest, which was evaluated in terms of Hp(10) and Hp(0.07) according to the plant's existing procedure.
>
> **Calibration**
>
> The dosimetry system was calibrated in terms of Hp(3) using a cylindrical phantom in accordance with ISO 4037-3. Reference irradiations were performed with a cesium-137 source in a secondary standards dosimetry laboratory whose air kerma standard is traceable to the national primary standard. Air kerma values were converted to Hp(3) using the conversion coefficients specified in the standard. Before field use, each detector was assigned an individual sensitivity correction factor determined from a uniform reference irradiation, and detectors whose response deviated by more than 10 percent from the batch mean were excluded.
>
> **Energy and angular response**
>
> Because the calibration field differed from the fields encountered in the plant, the energy and angular response of the dosimeter was verified at additional reference qualities, including cobalt-60 gamma radiation and a strontium-90/yttrium-90 beta field, at angles of incidence from 0° to 60°. Responses relative to the calibration field remained within the limits specified in IEC 62387 for the relevant energy and angular ranges.
>
> **Readout and dose evaluation**
>
> Dosimeters were read out using an automated reader with a linear heating profile. Signals were corrected for background using unirradiated control dosimeters stored at the plant during the monitoring period, and for individual sensitivity. The detection limit was defined as three times the standard deviation of the control dosimeter readings, converted to dose.
>
> **Uncertainty**
>
> The combined standard uncertainty of each Hp(3) value was evaluated following the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM). Components included the calibration of the reference field, reproducibility of the reader, individual sensitivity correction, energy and angular dependence in the field, and background subtraction. Expanded uncertainties are reported with a coverage factor of k = 2, corresponding to a coverage probability of approximately 95 percent.

**⑮ Expanded uncertainties are reported with a coverage factor of k = 2, corresponding to a coverage probability of approximately 95 percent.**
확장 불확도는 포함 인자 k = 2로 보고하며, 이는 약 95%의 포함 확률에 해당한다.
어휘: expanded uncertainty 확장 불확도 · coverage factor 포함 인자 · correspond to ~에 해당하다 · coverage probability 포함 확률

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 측정 방법 절의 구성

| 소제목 | 독자의 질문 | 이 글의 핵심 문장 |
|---|---|---|
| Dosimeters and positioning | 무엇으로, 어디에서 쟀는가? | Lens doses **were measured using**… positioned at the side of the head closest to the main radiation source |
| Calibration | 측정값을 무엇에 기대어 믿을 수 있는가? | calibrated in terms of Hp(3)… **in accordance with** ISO 4037-3 / **traceable to** the national primary standard |
| Energy and angular response | 교정 조건과 현장 조건의 차이는? | **Because** the calibration field **differed from** the fields encountered in the plant, … **was verified** |
| Readout and dose evaluation | 신호를 어떻게 선량으로 바꿨는가? | Signals **were corrected for** background… The detection limit **was defined as**… |
| Uncertainty | 측정값은 얼마나 정확한가? | **following** the GUM… Expanded uncertainties **are reported with** a coverage factor of k = 2 |

**3문단이 이 절의 핵심입니다.** 교정 조건(Cs-137, 정면 입사)이 현장 조건(Co-60 위주, 다양한 각도, 베타선)과 다르다는 점을 먼저 밝히고, 그 차이를 어떻게 확인했는지 서술합니다. 심사자가 가장 먼저 물을 질문에 미리 답하는 구조입니다.

### 2. 소급성 사슬 서술

> Reference irradiations were performed… in a secondary standards dosimetry laboratory **whose air kerma standard is traceable to** the national primary standard. Air kerma values **were converted to** Hp(3) **using the conversion coefficients specified in** the standard.

| 고리 | 표현 |
|---|---|
| 국가 일차 표준 | the national primary standard |
| → 이차 표준 실험실 | a secondary standards dosimetry laboratory whose… is traceable to… |
| → 기준장의 공기 커마 | air kerma values |
| → 실용량 | were converted to Hp(3) using the conversion coefficients |
| → 개별 검출기 | each detector was assigned an individual sensitivity correction factor |

### 3. 방법 절의 시제

방법 절은 **과거 수동태**가 기본입니다(were measured, was calibrated, were excluded). 다만 마지막 문장의 "Expanded uncertainties **are reported**"는 이 논문 안에서 보고하는 방식을 말하므로 **현재시제**입니다. 표준 문서의 내용("specified in the standard")이나 실험실의 지속적 상태("is traceable")도 현재시제를 씁니다.
