## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> The lens of the eye is among the most radiosensitive tissues in the human body, and its protection has become a renewed focus of occupational radiation protection over the past decade. In 2011, the International Commission on Radiological Protection (ICRP) recommended a substantial reduction in the occupational equivalent dose limit for the lens, from 150 mSv per year to 20 mSv per year averaged over defined five-year periods, with no single year exceeding 50 mSv. This recommendation has since been incorporated into a number of regulatory frameworks, including the European Basic Safety Standards adopted in 2013.
>
> The revision reflected accumulating epidemiological evidence that radiation-induced lens opacities may occur at lower doses than previously assumed. Studies of populations such as Chernobyl recovery workers and medical staff involved in interventional procedures reported an increased prevalence of lens changes at doses below the earlier threshold estimates. On the basis of such findings, the ICRP revised its nominal threshold for radiation-induced cataract to approximately 0.5 Gy and acknowledged that the latency of lens effects may be long.
>
> Implementing the new limit requires reliable estimation of lens dose in practice. The operational quantity recommended for this purpose is the personal dose equivalent at a depth of 3 mm, Hp(3). In many workplaces, however, lens dose has historically been estimated from dosimeters worn on the trunk and calibrated in terms of Hp(10) or Hp(0.07), on the assumption that these quantities provide adequate surrogates. Whether this assumption holds depends on the radiation field, in particular on the energy and angular distribution of photons and the presence of beta radiation. A dosimeter worn on the chest, for instance, may be partly shielded from radiation incident from above or from the side, or may receive a different dose from the eyes when the worker bends over a component.
>
> Considerable research has examined lens doses in medical settings, where interventional cardiologists and radiologists may receive substantial exposures to scattered low-energy photons. Fewer studies have addressed nuclear power plants, where radiation fields are dominated by higher-energy photons from activation products such as cobalt-60 and, in specific tasks, may include beta radiation from contaminated surfaces. Moreover, protective equipment used in nuclear power plants, such as full-face respirators, may itself alter the dose received by the lens. Consequently, it remains unclear whether existing trunk-worn dosimetry provides an adequate basis for demonstrating compliance with the revised lens limit in maintenance work.
>
> The present study addresses this question by comparing Hp(3) measured near the eyes with routinely recorded whole-body doses for workers in selected high-exposure tasks.

**⑨ In many workplaces, however, lens dose has historically been estimated from dosimeters worn on the trunk and calibrated in terms of Hp(10) or Hp(0.07), on the assumption that these quantities provide adequate surrogates.**
그러나 많은 작업장에서 수정체 선량은 관행적으로 몸통에 착용하고 Hp(10)이나 Hp(0.07)로 교정한 선량계로 추정해 왔는데, 이는 이 양들이 적절한 대리 지표가 된다는 가정에 따른 것이다.
어휘: workplace 작업장 · historically 역사적으로, 관행적으로 · dosimeter 선량계 · trunk 몸통 · calibrate 교정하다 · assumption 가정 · adequate 적절한 · surrogate 대리 지표

**⑩ Whether this assumption holds depends on the radiation field, in particular on the energy and angular distribution of photons and the presence of beta radiation.**
이 가정이 성립하는지는 방사선장, 특히 광자의 에너지와 입사 각도 분포, 그리고 베타선의 존재 여부에 달려 있다.
어휘: hold 성립하다 · radiation field 방사선장 · angular 각도의 · distribution 분포 · photon 광자 · presence 존재 · beta radiation 베타선

**⑪ A dosimeter worn on the chest, for instance, may be partly shielded from radiation incident from above or from the side, or may receive a different dose from the eyes when the worker bends over a component.**
예를 들어 가슴에 단 선량계는 위나 옆에서 입사하는 방사선에 대해 부분적으로 차폐될 수 있고, 작업자가 기기 위로 몸을 숙일 때는 눈과 다른 선량을 받을 수 있다.
어휘: chest 가슴 · shield 차폐하다 · incident 입사하는 · bend over 몸을 숙이다 · component 부품, 기기

### 문단 4

**⑫ Considerable research has examined lens doses in medical settings, where interventional cardiologists and radiologists may receive substantial exposures to scattered low-energy photons.**
중재 심장내과 의사와 영상의학과 의사가 산란된 저에너지 광자에 상당히 피폭될 수 있는 의료 환경의 수정체 선량에 대해서는 많은 연구가 이루어졌다.
어휘: considerable 상당한 · examine 살펴보다 · setting 환경 · cardiologist 심장내과 의사 · radiologist 영상의학과 의사 · exposure 피폭 · scattered 산란된

**⑬ Fewer studies have addressed nuclear power plants, where radiation fields are dominated by higher-energy photons from activation products such as cobalt-60 and, in specific tasks, may include beta radiation from contaminated surfaces.**
반면 원자력발전소를 다룬 연구는 더 적다. 원전의 방사선장은 코발트-60 같은 방사화 생성물에서 나오는 고에너지 광자가 지배적이며, 특정 작업에서는 오염된 표면에서 나오는 베타선이 포함될 수 있다.
어휘: address 다루다 · nuclear power plant 원자력발전소 · dominate 지배하다 · activation product 방사화 생성물 · task 작업 · contaminated 오염된

**⑭ Moreover, protective equipment used in nuclear power plants, such as full-face respirators, may itself alter the dose received by the lens.**
게다가 전면형 호흡 보호구처럼 원전에서 쓰는 보호 장비 자체가 수정체가 받는 선량을 바꿀 수도 있다.
어휘: protective equipment 보호 장비 · respirator 호흡 보호구 · alter 바꾸다

**⑮ Consequently, it remains unclear whether existing trunk-worn dosimetry provides an adequate basis for demonstrating compliance with the revised lens limit in maintenance work.**
그 결과, 원전 정비 작업에서 기존의 몸통 착용 선량 측정이 개정된 수정체 한도의 준수를 입증하는 적절한 근거가 되는지는 여전히 불분명하다.
어휘: consequently 결과적으로 · unclear 불분명한 · dosimetry 선량 측정 · basis 근거 · demonstrate 입증하다 · compliance 준수 · maintenance 정비

### 문단 5

**⑯ The present study addresses this question by comparing Hp(3) measured near the eyes with routinely recorded whole-body doses for workers in selected high-exposure tasks.**
본 연구는 선정된 고피폭 작업 작업자를 대상으로, 눈 근처에서 측정한 Hp(3)과 일상적으로 기록되는 전신 선량을 비교해 이 질문을 다룬다.
어휘: present 본, 현재의 · compare 비교하다 · routinely 일상적으로 · whole-body 전신의 · select 선정하다 · high-exposure 고피폭

### 1. CARS 모형 (Swales, 1990)

연구 논문 서론의 표준 구조입니다. "Create a Research Space"의 약자로, 서론은 연구가 들어설 자리를 만드는 과정으로 볼 수 있습니다.

| Move | 기능 | 하위 단계 |
|---|---|---|
| **Move 1. 영역 설정** (establishing a territory) | 이 주제가 왜 중요한지 보여 줌 | 중요성 주장 → 배경 일반화 → 선행 연구 검토 |
| Move 2. 공백 설정 (establishing a niche) | 기존 연구가 무엇을 놓쳤는지 제시 | 반론 제기 / 공백 지적 / 질문 제기 / 전통의 연장 |
| Move 3. 공백 점유 (occupying the niche) | 본 연구가 그 공백을 어떻게 채우는지 | 목적 진술 → 연구 질문 → 주요 결과 → 논문 구성 |
