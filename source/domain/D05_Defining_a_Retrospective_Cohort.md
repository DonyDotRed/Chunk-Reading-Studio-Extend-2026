## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> **Study design and data source**
>
> This retrospective cohort study used electronic health records from the endocrinology outpatient clinic of a tertiary hospital, linked with data exported from a CGM data-management platform. The study protocol was approved by the institutional review board, which waived the requirement for informed consent because only de-identified existing data were analyzed.
>
> **Participants**
>
> Adults aged 18 years or older with a diagnosis of type 2 diabetes who used CGM between January 2021 and December 2023 were screened. Eligible participants were required to have at least 14 days of CGM data with sensor wear time of 70 percent or more, in line with international consensus recommendations, and an HbA1c measurement obtained within 30 days after the end of the CGM period. Participants were excluded if they were pregnant, had an estimated glomerular filtration rate below 30 mL/min/1.73 m², had a hemoglobin concentration below 10 g/dL, had a known hemoglobin variant, or had received a blood transfusion within the preceding three months, since each of these conditions may distort the relationship between HbA1c and mean glucose. For participants with multiple eligible CGM periods, only the first was analyzed.
>
> **Exposure definitions**
>
> TIR was calculated as the percentage of CGM readings between 70 and 180 mg/dL. Concordance was defined using commonly applied targets: participants with HbA1c below 7.0 percent and TIR above 70 percent, or with HbA1c of 7.0 percent or higher and TIR of 70 percent or lower, were classified as concordant. The remaining participants were classified as discordant, distinguishing those with favorable HbA1c but low TIR from those with elevated HbA1c but high TIR. As a secondary measure, the difference between laboratory HbA1c and the glucose management indicator (GMI), an HbA1c-like estimate calculated from mean CGM glucose, was computed.
>
> **Covariates and outcomes**
>
> Covariates included age, sex, diabetes duration, body mass index, eGFR, hemoglobin concentration, and glucose-lowering therapy, categorized as insulin, sulfonylurea, or neither. The prognostic outcome was incident microvascular complication during follow-up, defined as new diabetic retinopathy on fundus examination or new albuminuria, defined as a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio of 30 mg/g or higher on at least two of three measurements.
>
> **Missing data**
>
> Covariates with missing values were handled by multiple imputation with chained equations, generating 20 imputed data sets; participants with missing exposure or outcome data were excluded from the relevant analyses.

예후 결과는 추적 관찰 중 새로 발생한 미세혈관 합병증으로, 안저 검사에서 새로 확인된 당뇨망막병증 또는 세 번의 측정 중 최소 두 번에서 소변 알부민/크레아티닌 비가 30 mg/g 이상인 새로운 알부민뇨로 정의했다.
어휘: prognostic 예후의 · outcome 결과 · incident 새로 발생한 · follow-up 추적 관찰 · fundus examination 안저 검사 · ratio 비

### 문단 5 · Missing data

**⑬ Covariates with missing values were handled by multiple imputation with chained equations, generating 20 imputed data sets; participants with missing exposure or outcome data were excluded from the relevant analyses.**
결측값이 있는 공변량은 연쇄 방정식을 이용한 다중 대체로 처리해 20개의 대체 자료 세트를 만들었고, 노출이나 결과 자료가 결측인 참여자는 해당 분석에서 제외했다.
어휘: missing value 결측값 · handle 처리하다 · multiple imputation 다중 대체 · chained equations 연쇄 방정식 · impute 대체하다 · relevant 해당하는

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 선정·제외 기준 쓰기

| 요소 | 표현 | 이 글의 예 |
|---|---|---|
| 선별 모집단 | … were screened | Adults aged 18 years or older… **were screened** |
| 선정 기준 | Eligible participants were required to have… | **were required to have** at least 14 days of CGM data |
| 근거 제시 | in line with… | **in line with** international consensus recommendations |
| 제외 기준 (병렬) | Participants were excluded if they… , had…, had…, or had… | **were excluded if they were** pregnant, **had** an eGFR…, **had** a hemoglobin…, **had** a known…, **or had received**… |
| 제외 근거 | since each of these conditions may… | **since each of these conditions may distort** the relationship… |
| 중복 처리 | only the first was analyzed | For participants with multiple eligible CGM periods, **only the first was analyzed** |

제외 기준은 병렬 구조(had…, had…, or had…)로 나열하고, 마지막에 **왜 제외했는지**를 한 번에 밝힙니다. 심사자는 제외 기준마다 "왜?"라고 묻기 때문에, 근거가 같은 기준은 묶어서 설명하는 것이 효율적입니다.

### 2. 조작적 정의

| 개념 | 조작적 정의 |
|---|---|
| TIR | the percentage of CGM readings between 70 and 180 mg/dL |
| 일치 / 불일치 | HbA1c < 7.0% and TIR > 70%, **or** HbA1c ≥ 7.0% and TIR ≤ 70% → concordant |
| 알부민뇨 | UACR ≥ 30 mg/g on at least two of three measurements |

"was defined as"와 "was calculated as"는 방법 절에서 가장 자주 쓰이는 표현입니다. 경계값을 다룰 때 **초과(above)와 이상(or higher)을 정확히 구분**해야 분류에서 빠지는 사람이 생기지 않습니다. 이 글의 정의는 HbA1c 7.0%와 TIR 70%를 경계로 네 조합을 빈틈없이 나눕니다.

### 3. 윤리 진술의 표준 형식

> The study protocol **was approved by the institutional review board, which waived the requirement for informed consent because** only de-identified existing data were analyzed.

승인 기관, 동의 면제 여부, 면제 사유를 한 문장에 담는 형식입니다. 실제 원고에는 승인 번호를 함께 적습니다.
