### 문단 2

**②** We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Discordance Between HbA1c and Time in Range and Risk of Microvascular Complications in Type 2 Diabetes: A Retrospective Cohort Study" for consideration as an original article in your journal.  
<sub>↳ We are pleased / to submit / our manuscript / entitled / "Discordance Between HbA1c / and Time in Range / and Risk of Microvascular Complications / in Type 2 Diabetes: / A Retrospective Cohort Study" / for consideration / as an original article / in your journal.</sub>

<sub>↳ HbA1c remains / the principal target / in diabetes care, / yet it may misrepresent / glycemic exposure / in some patients.</sub>

**④** In a cohort of 1,126 adults with type 2 diabetes using continuous glucose monitoring in routine care, we found that one in four showed discordance between HbA1c and time in range.  
<sub>↳ In a cohort / of 1,126 adults / with type 2 diabetes / using continuous glucose monitoring / in routine care, / we found / that one in four / showed discordance / between HbA1c / and time in range.</sub>
