## Part A. 역번역 문제

### 문단 1 · What is this study about?

**①** 당뇨병이 있는 사람들은 흔히 HbA1c라는 혈액 검사를 받습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (12단어): —</sub>  
✍️ 

**②** 이 검사는 지난 2–3개월 동안의 평균 혈당을 보여 줍니다.  
<sub>힌트 (14단어): blood sugar 혈당</sub>  
✍️ 

**③** 요즘은 피부에 작은 센서를 붙여 밤낮으로 혈당을 재는 사람도 많습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (19단어): sensor 센서</sub>  
✍️ 

**④** 의사는 이 센서로 하루 중 얼마나 오래 혈당이 건강한 범위에 머무는지 볼 수 있습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (18단어): range 범위</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑤** 이것을 "목표 범위 내 시간"이라고 합니다.  
<sub>힌트 (6단어): —</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑥** 저희는 이 두 측정값이 서로 맞지 않을 때 어떤 일이 생기는지 알고 싶었습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (11단어): measure 측정값 · disagree 일치하지 않다</sub>  
✍️ 

### 문단 2 · What did we do?

**⑦** 평소 진료의 일환으로 센서를 사용한 제2형 당뇨병 성인 1,126명의 의무 기록을 살펴보았습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (24단어): medical record 의무 기록</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑧** 이들의 HbA1c와 목표 범위 내 시간을 비교했고, 거의 3년 동안 추적하며 누구에게 당뇨병으로 인한 눈이나 콩팥 문제가 새로 생기는지 보았습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (31단어): follow 추적 관찰하다 · kidney 콩팥</sub>  
✍️ 

### 문단 3 · What did we find?

**⑨** 약 네 명 중 한 명은 두 결과가 서로 맞지 않았습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (11단어): match 일치하다</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑩** 가장 중요한 집단은 HbA1c는 좋아 보였지만 혈당이 건강한 범위에 머문 시간이 너무 적었던 사람들이었습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (22단어): —</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑪** 이 사람들은 두 결과가 모두 좋았던 사람들보다 눈이나 콩팥 문제가 새로 생길 가능성이 더 높았습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (19단어): be likely to ~할 가능성이 있다</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑫** 이 집단의 사람들은 인슐린을 쓰는 경우가 많았고, 일부는 산소를 나르는 혈액 성분인 혈색소 수치가 낮았습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (23단어): insulin 인슐린 · hemoglobin 혈색소 · oxygen 산소</sub>  
✍️ 

### 문단 4 · What does this mean?

**⑬** HbA1c 결과가 좋다고 해서 늘 모든 것을 말해 주는 것은 아닙니다.  
<sub>힌트 (11단어): —</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑭** 어떤 사람, 특히 인슐린을 쓰는 사람에게는 센서 자료가 HbA1c가 놓치는 문제를 보여 줄 수 있습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (15단어): miss 놓치다</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑮** 이 연구는 연관을 보여 줄 뿐 한쪽이 다른 쪽의 원인이라는 증거는 아니며, 한 병원에서만 진행되었습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (20단어): link 연관 · proof 증거</sub>  
✍️ 

**⑯** 센서를 쓰고 계신다면, HbA1c뿐 아니라 목표 범위 내 시간에 대해서도 의사와 이야기해 보시면 좋겠습니다.  
<sub>힌트 (23단어): worth -ing ~할 가치가 있는 · as well as ~뿐 아니라</sub>

## Part D. 정답과 섀도잉 스크립트 (풀이 후 확인)

> 끊어 읽기 표시( / )는 1회차 청크 경계입니다. 섀도잉은 음성 없이 시작해도 됩니다. 먼저 끊어 읽기 단위로 소리 내어 읽고, 익숙해지면 표시를 무시하고 한 호흡에 읽으세요.

### 문단 1 · What is this study about?

**①** People with diabetes are often checked with a blood test called HbA1c.  
<sub>↳ People with diabetes / are often checked / with a blood test / called HbA1c.</sub>

**②** It shows the average blood sugar level over the past two to three months.  
<sub>↳ It shows / the average / blood sugar level / over the past / two to three months.</sub>

**③** Many people now also wear a small sensor on their skin that measures blood sugar all day and night.  
<sub>↳ Many people / now also wear / a small sensor / on their skin / that measures blood sugar / all day and night.</sub>

**④** From this sensor, doctors can see how much of the day blood sugar stays in a healthy range.  
<sub>↳ From this sensor, / doctors can see / how much of the day / blood sugar stays / in a healthy range.</sub>

**⑤** This is called "time in range."  
<sub>↳ This is called / "time in range."</sub>

**⑥** We wanted to know what happens when these two measures disagree.  
<sub>↳ We wanted to know / what happens / when these two measures / disagree.</sub>

### 문단 2 · What did we do?

**⑦** We looked at the medical records of 1,126 adults with type 2 diabetes who had used a sensor as part of their usual care.  
<sub>↳ We looked at / the medical records / of 1,126 adults / with type 2 diabetes / who had used a sensor / as part of / their usual care.</sub>

**⑧** We compared their HbA1c with their time in range, and we followed them for almost three years to see who developed new problems in the eyes or kidneys caused by diabetes.  
<sub>↳ We compared / their HbA1c / with their time in range, / and we followed them / for almost three years / to see / who developed / new problems / in the eyes or kidneys / caused by diabetes.</sub>

### 문단 3 · What did we find?

**⑨** About one in four people had results that did not match.  
<sub>↳ About one in four people / had results / that did not match.</sub>

**⑩** The most important group was people whose HbA1c looked good but whose blood sugar spent too little time in the healthy range.  
<sub>↳ The most important group / was people / whose HbA1c looked good / but whose blood sugar / spent too little time / in the healthy range.</sub>

**⑪** These people were more likely to develop new eye or kidney problems than people whose results were both good.  
<sub>↳ These people / were more likely / to develop / new eye or kidney problems / than people / whose results / were both good.</sub>

**⑫** People in this group were often using insulin, and some had lower levels of hemoglobin, the part of the blood that carries oxygen.  
<sub>↳ People in this group / were often using insulin, / and some had / lower levels / of hemoglobin, / the part of the blood / that carries oxygen.</sub>

### 문단 4 · What does this mean?

**⑬** A good HbA1c result may not always tell the whole story.  
<sub>↳ A good HbA1c result / may not always tell / the whole story.</sub>

**⑭** For some people, especially those using insulin, sensor data may show problems that HbA1c misses.  
<sub>↳ For some people, / especially those / using insulin, / sensor data / may show problems / that HbA1c misses.</sub>

**⑮** This study shows a link, not proof that one thing causes another, and it was done at a single hospital.  
<sub>↳ This study / shows a link, / not proof / that one thing / causes another, / and it was done / at a single hospital.</sub>

**⑯** If you use a sensor, it may be worth talking with your doctor about your time in range as well as your HbA1c.  
<sub>↳ If you use a sensor, / it may be worth / talking with your doctor / about your time in range / as well as your HbA1c.</sub>
