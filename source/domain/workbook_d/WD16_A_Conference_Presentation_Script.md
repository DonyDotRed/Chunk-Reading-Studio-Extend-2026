## 진행 순서

1. **Part A** 한국어 문장을 보고 영어로 씁니다. 처음에는 힌트를 보며 풀고, 복습 때는 힌트를 가리고 풉니다.
2. **Part D** 정답과 비교해 **Part C**에 점수(0–3)와 오류 유형 코드를 적습니다. 채점 기준은 `00W_Backtranslation_Guide.md`에 있습니다.
3. **Part D**의 끊어 읽기 스크립트로 정답 문장을 섀도잉합니다.
4. **Part B** 리텔링을 원문을 보지 않고 수행합니다.
5. 1일·3일·7일·21일 뒤에 2점 이하 문장만 다시 풉니다.

> 한국어 번역은 직역이 아니라 자연스러운 의역입니다. 정답과 문장 구조가 달라도 의미와 핵심 어휘가 맞으면 높은 점수를 주세요.

## Part B. 리텔링 과제

원문을 보지 않고 아래 구조에 맞춰 **영어로 4–5문장** 말하거나 쓰세요. 학술 문체(완곡 표현, 출처를 밝히는 동사, 명사화)를 의식적으로 사용합니다.

| 단계 | 내용 | 문장 수 |
|---|---|---|
| 논제 | 이 글이 주장하거나 설명하는 핵심 | 1 |
| 전개 | 핵심 근거, 개념, 또는 분석 단계 | 2–3 |
| 마무리 | 한계·단서 또는 결론 | 1 |

**필수 사용 어휘** (5개 이상 사용):

as many of you know (많은 분들이 아시다시피) · in other words (다시 말해) · the picture is less clear (상황이 덜 분명하다) · point to (~을 가리키다) · to find out (알아보기 위해) · I should stress that (~을 강조하고 싶다) · here's the main result (주요 결과입니다) · makes sense (타당하다) · as you can see (보시다시피) · as long as (~하는 한) · line of equality (등가선) · in a row (연이어)

## Part D. 정답과 섀도잉 스크립트 (풀이 후 확인)

> 끊어 읽기 표시( / )는 1회차 청크 경계입니다. 섀도잉은 음성 없이 시작해도 됩니다. 먼저 끊어 읽기 단위로 소리 내어 읽고, 익숙해지면 표시를 무시하고 한 호흡에 읽으세요.

### 문단 1 · Slide 1 · Title

**①** Good morning, everyone.  
<sub>↳ Good morning, everyone.</sub>

**②** My name is [name], and I work on occupational dosimetry at a nuclear power research institute.  
<sub>↳ My name is [name], / and I work / on occupational dosimetry / at a nuclear power / research institute.</sub>

**③** Today I'd like to share a simple question we tried to answer: can the dosimeter on a worker's chest tell us enough about the dose to their eyes?  
<sub>↳ Today / I'd like to share / a simple question / we tried to answer: / can the dosimeter / on a worker's chest / tell us enough / about the dose / to their eyes?</sub>

### 문단 2 · Slide 2 · Why this matters

**④** As many of you know, in 2011 the ICRP lowered the occupational limit for the lens of the eye from 150 to 20 millisieverts per year, averaged over five years.  
<sub>↳ As many of you know, / in 2011 / the ICRP lowered / the occupational limit / for the lens of the eye / from 150 / to 20 millisieverts per year, / averaged over five years.</sub>

**⑤** That's a reduction of more than eighty-five percent.  
<sub>↳ That's a reduction / of more than / eighty-five percent.</sub>

**⑥** In hospitals, this has meant new eye dosimeters for interventional staff.  
<sub>↳ In hospitals, / this has meant / new eye dosimeters / for interventional staff.</sub>

**⑦** But for nuclear power plants, the picture is less clear.  
<sub>↳ But for nuclear power plants, / the picture / is less clear.</sub>

**⑧** Our fields are dominated by high-energy photons, and in some tasks there's beta radiation as well.  
<sub>↳ Our fields / are dominated / by high-energy photons, / and in some tasks / there's beta radiation / as well.</sub>

**⑨** So the question is: do we really need dedicated eye dosimeters for everyone?  
<sub>↳ So the question is: / do we really need / dedicated eye dosimeters / for everyone?</sub>

### 문단 3 · Slide 3 · What we did

**⑩** To find out, we monitored forty-two workers during one refueling outage.  
<sub>↳ To find out, / we monitored / forty-two workers / during one refueling outage.</sub>

**⑪** Each worker wore an eye lens dosimeter on a headband, calibrated for Hp(3), together with their usual chest dosimeter.  
<sub>↳ Each worker wore / an eye lens dosimeter / on a headband, / calibrated for Hp(3), / together with / their usual chest dosimeter.</sub>

**⑫** That gave us one hundred and sixty-eight paired measurements across six types of maintenance work.  
<sub>↳ That gave us / one hundred and sixty-eight / paired measurements / across six types / of maintenance work.</sub>

### 문단 4 · Slide 4 · Main result

**⑬** Here's the main result.  
<sub>↳ Here's the main result.</sub>

**⑭** On this plot, each dot is one pair: eye dose on the vertical axis, chest dose on the horizontal axis.  
<sub>↳ On this plot, / each dot / is one pair: / eye dose / on the vertical axis, / chest dose / on the horizontal axis.</sub>

**⑮** As you can see, most of the dots sit very close to the line of equality.  
<sub>↳ As you can see, / most of the dots / sit very close / to the line of equality.</sub>

**⑯** The median ratio of Hp(3) to Hp(10) was 1.08.  
<sub>↳ The median ratio / of Hp(3) to Hp(10) / was 1.08.</sub>

**⑰** In other words, for most tasks, the chest dosimeter gave a good estimate of the eye dose, just slightly low, by about eight percent on average.  
<sub>↳ In other words, / for most tasks, / the chest dosimeter / gave a good estimate / of the eye dose, / just slightly low, / by about eight percent / on average.</sub>

### 문단 5 · Slide 5 · The exceptions

**⑱** But there were exceptions, and they're interesting.  
<sub>↳ But there were exceptions, / and they're interesting.</sub>

**⑲** In six pairs, the eye dose was more than one and a half times the chest dose.  
<sub>↳ In six pairs, / the eye dose / was more than / one and a half times / the chest dose.</sub>

**⑳** All six came from valve maintenance near contaminated surfaces.  
<sub>↳ All six / came from valve maintenance / near contaminated surfaces.</sub>

**㉑** And in all six, the shallow dose, Hp(0.07), was also high.
