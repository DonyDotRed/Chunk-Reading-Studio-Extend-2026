## 0. 원문 (Full Text)

> Dear Editor,
>
> We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Discordance Between HbA1c and Time in Range and Risk of Microvascular Complications in Type 2 Diabetes: A Retrospective Cohort Study" for consideration as an original article in your journal.
>
> HbA1c remains the principal target in diabetes care, yet it may misrepresent glycemic exposure in some patients. In a cohort of 1,126 adults with type 2 diabetes using continuous glucose monitoring in routine care, we found that one in four showed discordance between HbA1c and time in range. Notably, patients whose HbA1c was at target but whose time in range was low had a higher adjusted hazard of incident microvascular complications (hazard ratio, 1.46; 95% CI, 1.02–2.09).
>
> We believe these findings will be of interest to your readership because they identify a group of patients who may be considered well controlled on the basis of HbA1c alone yet remain at increased risk. They also suggest a practical role for CGM-derived metrics in routine assessment, an issue of growing relevance as CGM use expands beyond insulin-treated populations.
>
> This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the final version and declare no competing interests. The study was approved by the institutional review board of the participating hospital, which waived the requirement for informed consent.
>
> Thank you for considering our manuscript. We look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Sincerely, On behalf of all co-authors, the corresponding author

## 학술 영어 포인트

### 1. 투고 서신의 구조

| 문단 | 기능 | 핵심 표현 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 제출 진술 (제목, 원고 유형) | **We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "…" for consideration as an original article in your journal.** |
| 2 | 문제와 핵심 결과 (초록보다 더 짧게) | **we found that**… **Notably**, … |
| 3 | 독자 적합성 (왜 이 학술지인가) | **We believe these findings will be of interest to your readership because**… |
| 4 | 행정 진술 (중복 투고, 저자 승인, 이해 상충, 윤리) | **has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere** / **declare no competing interests** |
| 5 | 감사와 마무리 | **Thank you for considering our manuscript. We look forward to hearing from you.** |

**3문단이 가장 중요합니다.** 편집자는 투고 서신을 보고 원고를 심사에 보낼지(desk rejection 여부) 먼저 판단하는 경우가 많습니다. "흥미로운 결과"라고만 쓰지 말고, **누구에게 왜 중요한지**를 구체적으로 써야 합니다.

### 2. 위험비를 일상어로 옮길 때의 정확성

| 부정확한 표현 | 이 글의 표현 | 이유 |
|---|---|---|
| had a 46% higher risk | had a **higher adjusted hazard** (hazard ratio, 1.46; 95% CI, 1.02–2.09) | 위험비(HR)는 순간 위험률의 비이며, 누적 위험(risk)의 비와 같지 않음 |
| doubled the risk | — | 과장 |
| proved that low TIR causes complications | — | 관찰 연구는 인과를 증명하지 않음 |

투고 서신은 연구를 "파는" 글이라 과장의 유혹이 큽니다. 그러나 편집자는 그 분야 전문가이므로, 과장은 오히려 신뢰를 떨어뜨립니다.

### 3. 제목 쓰기: 세 가지 유형

| 유형 | 예 | 특징 |
|---|---|---|
| 서술형 (descriptive) | Discordance Between HbA1c and Time in Range and Risk of Microvascular Complications in Type 2 Diabetes: A Retrospective Cohort Study | 무엇을 연구했는지. 임상 학술지에서 가장 흔하며, 콜론 뒤에 연구 설계를 명시 |
| 선언형 (declarative) | Low Time in Range Despite Target HbA1c Is Associated with Microvascular Complications in Type 2 Diabetes | 결과를 문장으로. 눈에 잘 띄지만 과장 위험 |
| 의문형 (interrogative) | Is HbA1c Enough? Time in Range and Microvascular Risk in Type 2 Diabetes | 관심을 끌지만 일부 학술지는 지양 |

이 서신은 서술형 제목을 썼습니다. 관찰 연구에서 선언형 제목을 쓸 때는 "causes" 대신 "is associated with"를 써야 합니다.

### 4. 키워드 고르기

| 제목에 이미 있는 단어는 가급적 피함 (검색 범위를 넓히기 위해) | 제목에 HbA1c, time in range가 있으므로 키워드는 continuous glucose monitoring, glycated hemoglobin, glucose management indicator, diabetic retinopathy, albuminuria 등 |
